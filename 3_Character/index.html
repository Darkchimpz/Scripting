Appearance

Malissa is from Gurin Mountain; a story about a simulation where 8 people 'spawn' in the middle of nowhere with little to no memories of a past life, but of the 8: Malissa seems to know way more than her peers.

Her design uses what is likely the 5 most common colors in character design: white, black, red, blue and yellow (technically skin-color as well). Her appearance is something ordinary in it's excellence; a visually appealing woman, but a very generic example of one. Her name: 'Malissa' is nice, but very ordinary. However: the high saturation and contrast and the slightly larger-than-normal eyes may put her in the uncanny valley. She almost matches the appearance of idealized femininity, but something isn't right.

Behaviour

Her appearance is meant to match her behaviour; going through the motions of her fellow characters, but deep down having motives, feelings and fears that are so detached from the others, she just can't help but feel contempt when she has to 'play along'. Furthermore: when she is 'playing along' but not really trying, it comes off as uncanny / unsettling because it implies she knows a lot more than the other characters, and that whatever she knows makes her indifferent to the same things that the regular characters see as threatening.

Her relationship with most of the other characters is that of 'former friend', however, they don't remember her. In Episode 2 (yet to be released) it's hinted that Malissa is Keith's ex (Keith is the protagonist), which partly explains her strange behaviour around him in Episode 1

Weird Things She's Done

When hunting a rabbit, she opts to finish it off by stomping on it (slow death) rather than a quicker more humane death. She doesn't care that the main character sees this because she already knows he's not the kind of person to mention it to anyone.

She addresses the main character by name, even though he hasn't yet introduced himself.
